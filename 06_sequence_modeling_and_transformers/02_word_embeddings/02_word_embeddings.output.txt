=== Part 1: WordNet -- a hand-built symbolic lookup ===
WordNet has 18 synsets for 'bank'. The first few:
  bank.n.01              sloping land (especially the slope beside a body of water)
  depository_financial_institution.n.01 a financial institution that accepts deposits and channels the money into lending activities
  bank.n.03              a long ridge or pile
  bank.n.04              an arrangement of similar objects in a row or in tiers
Each sense is a separate, hand-written entry -- a lexicographer decided these are different meanings.

=== Part 2: GloVe -- a learned distributional vector ===
Predict-before-reveal: 'bank' has a riverbank sense and a financial sense. Will GloVe's nearest neighbors show both, or just one?
GloVe's 6 nearest neighbors to 'bank' (one vector, no senses):
  securities cosine similarity 0.800
  banking    cosine similarity 0.797
  investment cosine similarity 0.785
  financial  cosine similarity 0.767
  credit     cosine similarity 0.765
  finance    cosine similarity 0.708
Every neighbor is financial. The riverbank sense doesn't even appear: whichever sense dominates the training text swallows the other one completely, because there is only one vector to hold both.
