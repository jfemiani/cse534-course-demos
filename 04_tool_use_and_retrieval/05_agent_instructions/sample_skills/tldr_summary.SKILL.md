# TL;DR summary

1. Read the whole piece before writing anything; do not summarize a text you have only skimmed.
2. Write exactly one sentence, no more than 25 words, capturing the single most important takeaway. Not the topic; the conclusion.
3. Put that sentence first, on its own line, labeled "TL;DR:".
4. Below it, keep the full original text unchanged. A TL;DR is added on top of the text, not a replacement for it.
5. If the piece makes more than one claim of comparable importance, pick the one the reader is most likely to act on, not the one stated first.
